How checks work & privacy

Availability comes from Apple’s public pickup service. Results refresh about every two minutes for everyone; the page updates its display every 30 seconds. Automatic area checks continue while this tab is open. Apple errors trigger a cooldown, and older results become unconfirmed. Texas directory verified October 27, 2026.

We only check availability. Purchases and reservations happen on Apple’s website. Confirm carrier eligibility and final stock there. Independent project; not affiliated with Apple. Your model preference stays in your browser; your searched ZIP is sent to this service and Apple to find nearby stores. We never ask for an Apple account. The checker is completely free, with no account or payment required.
