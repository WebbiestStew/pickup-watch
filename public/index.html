How checks work & privacy

Availability comes from Apple’s public pickup service. Free results are shared for up to 15 minutes; Plus results refresh about every two minutes; the page updates its display every 30 seconds. Automatic area checks continue while this tab is open. Apple errors trigger a cooldown, and older results become unconfirmed. Nationwide directory verified October 27, 2026.

We only check availability. Purchases and reservations happen on Apple’s website. Confirm carrier eligibility and final stock there. Independent project; not affiliated with Apple. Your model preference stays in your browser; your searched ZIP is sent to this service and Apple to find nearby stores. We never ask for an Apple account. Optional Plus payments are handled on Stripe’s checkout page; this site does not collect card details.
